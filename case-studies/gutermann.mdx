# Gutermann Cloud Application

## Overview

**Gutermann Cloud** is the browser-based platform behind Gutermann's correlating IoT leak detection systems. Utilities use it to manage acoustic logger fleets, visualise leaks on map and Street View interfaces, run automatic correlation and AI leak scoring, and turn alarms into repair workflows.

---

## My Role

Full-stack / cloud application development across the map-centric operator experience, event workflows, integrations with the logger ecosystem, and secure multi-tenant cloud delivery.

---

## The Challenge

Water utilities run large networks of acoustic IoT loggers. Operators needed one cloud workspace — not desktop-only tools or fragmented spreadsheets — to see infrastructure on maps, analyse correlation and spectrum data, classify alarms, and drive repairs from anywhere.

---

## Highlights

### Map-first operations

Geospatial display of loggers and leaks with Google Maps and Street View, plus KML GIS import for network-specific pipe data.

### Correlation and AI scoring

Automatic daily correlation, advanced spectrum analysis to reduce false alarms, and AI-assisted leak probability scoring.

### Event workflows

Ticket management for active and repaired leaks, report generation, email alerting, and remote specialist access for difficult investigations.

---

## Outcome

A single cloud application for permanent leak monitoring — clearer triage, stronger false-alarm control, and worldwide remote access for utility operators and Gutermann specialists.
